Zoom tiers

  • Full, zoom ≥ 0.864 (11 px × z ≥ 9.5): everything drawn.
  • Compact, zoom < 0.864: text is held at 9.5 screen px instead of hidden. Labels switch to short form; a label is dropped only when the short form is wider than its slot. Values and captions hide (shown on hover and drag).
  • Overview, zoom < 0.55 (proposed, not drawn in R1): title, display, connected jacks only.

Today labels vanish below 86 %, so a plugin window at 65–75 % shows no labels at all. Compact fixes that without breaking the 9.5 px legibility rule.

Checker rules (run on this page)

  • overlap: any two leaf boxes (text, knob, jack, selector, chip, pad) intersect.
  • gap: leaves closer than 3 px (2 px for a label to its own control), screen px.
  • edge: leaf closer than 4 px to the face edge; child outside its container.
  • fit: text wider than its slot after short-name fallback (would need ellipsis).
  • missing: a required label not drawn at Full tier.
  • contrast: text pair under 4.5:1.
  • Info: text dropped by Compact rules (expected, listed for review).

All geometry-only, so the same checks port to rack.rs unit tests with no GPU.

Decisions taken in R1

  • Direction: evolve Seated. Depth stays (seated wells, proud hardware); wells now take the face hue.
  • Themes: one material (Satin), light and dark, done properly. Lacquer returns later as a token set once Satin is signed off.
  • FM Voice: operator cards show ratio, level and an envelope curve. Fine and velocity per operator go behind +12.
  • Sequencer: 32 steps as 4 pages of 8; page selector in the header beside edit and play banks.
  • Drum grid: velocity = fill height, chance = pips. Readable without colour.
  • Stereo: double-ring jack, twin-strand cable.

Open for your review
