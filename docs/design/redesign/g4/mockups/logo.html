Recommendation

  • Keep your logo's idea; drop my tile placeholder. Yours is the stronger concept: the name is literally a cable, and the plugs say "patch" before anyone reads the word. My orange S-curve tile was a stand-in.
  • Refine, don't replace. Your letters and your k stay exactly as drawn (copied from your SVG paths). Only the loop and plugs are rebuilt: one even frame weight, true arcs, plugs with clear gaps between body, sleeve, ring and tip. The loop is a little roomier so the letters breathe.
  • Three files, one family: lockup (toolbar, About, splash), detailed mark (32 px and up), simplified mark (16–24 px: plugs become two blocks, since plug detail turns to mush below 32 px).
  • Colour: logo drawn in the text colour in the toolbar (calm, both themes). Orange only for the app icon tile and splash, where the brand should shout.
  • Your call: rebuilt frame and plugs, or your original exactly as it is. The letters are identical in both.
