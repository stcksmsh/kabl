# A held lead note, then the controller is unplugged: its notes must be released.
midi on 76 110

shot r01-note-held

# Plugged back in: reconnects by itself, every mapping waits for pickup again.
midistart

# Pick the Bass level up and turn it; a mouse edit on it; the hardware must not jump it back.

sleep 0.5
midi cc 1 20 41
sleep 0.5
