# Name and logo policy

Draft for the owner's decision. Not legal advice.

The name "kabl" and the kabl logo belong to the owner named in `COPYRIGHT`. The GPL-3.0 licence of
the code gives no right to either (`COPYRIGHT`, term (e)). The logo files are also protected by
copyright and are not under the GPL (`ASSET-LICENSES.md`).

## You may

- Say that your work works with, is made with or is based on kabl, in plain text, as long as it is true.
- Use the name when you distribute an unmodified kabl build or source obtained from the project.
- Keep the logo files unchanged inside a copy of the kabl source tree or an unmodified kabl package.
- Write about kabl, review it, link to it, show screenshots of it.
- Fork the code and sell or give away copies under the GPL-3.0.

## You may not

- Call a modified version "kabl", or use a name, logo, icon or domain that can be mistaken for kabl.
- Ship a modified version with the kabl logo, mark or icon as its own branding.
- Suggest that the owner made, approved or supports your version.
- Register "kabl" or a similar name as a trademark, domain or app-store name.

## A fork must

- Use another name and its own logo.
- Keep the attribution "Based on kabl by <OWNER NAME>, https://github.com/stcksmsh/kabl" in its
  README and in its About or credits screen (`COPYRIGHT`, term (b)).
- Mark itself as modified (`COPYRIGHT`, term (c)).

## What a licence can and cannot enforce

The GPL-3.0 allows the owner to add only the terms of its section 7: keep legal notices and
author attributions (b), mark modified versions (c), not use the authors' names for publicity
(d), and decline to grant trademark rights (e). `COPYRIGHT` uses those four and nothing else.

A licence cannot:

- make "must not use the name" binding on its own. That comes from trademark law (unregistered
  use, and much more strongly a registration), not from the licence. Registration is not done.
- forbid commercial forks, selling copies, or a fork's use of the code in other free software. The
  GPL allows all of it.
- stop someone who never agreed to anything from naming kabl truthfully. That is nominative use.
- require the attribution line in places that are not part of a distribution (a fork's website).
