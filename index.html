03 Functional cables

A cable is more than a wire. It can carry a step pattern, a pass probability, an A/B morph and glide, all locked to the patch clock, and macros can drive cable parameters.

A
B

A diagram of the idea, not kabl's audio engine. Each pulse of the clock (a 16th note) moves one step: the pulse passes with the cable's chance, its level is the A step blended toward the B step by Morph, and Glide slews between levels. In kabl each step also has its own level and chance; here a step is simply on or off.

Clock-locked. Macro-addressable. Details: docs/functional-cables

05 Build & run

From source

Needs a current stable Rust toolchain and, on Debian or Ubuntu, libasound2-dev libxkbcommon-dev libdbus-1-dev.

cargo run --release -p kabl-ui        # the standalone synth
cargo build --release -p kabl-clap    # the plugin: target/release/libkabl_clap.so
cargo test --workspace

From a release tarball

When a release is published on the Releases page, download kabl-<version>-linux-<arch>.tar.gz, then:

tar -xzf kabl-*-linux-*.tar.gz
cd kabl-*-linux-*
./install.sh          # standalone, desktop entry, plugin in ~/.clap
./install.sh --uninstall

Runtime needs ALSA (libasound2), X11 or Wayland with OpenGL, and libxkbcommon-x11. In a DAW, scan ~/.clap and load kabl as an instrument. Your own sounds are saved to ~/.local/share/kabl/sounds and are never touched by an upgrade or an uninstall.
